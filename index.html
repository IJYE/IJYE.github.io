HOW SUBMISSION WORKS

Evidence first.
Then review.

Submissions should make it possible to understand what was built, why it was built that way, and how the result was evaluated.

  1. 01

    Document the work

    Prepare a clear manuscript with the problem, approach, implementation, results, and limitations.

  2. 02

    Include evidence

    Where relevant, provide a repository, diagrams, test results, demonstrations, or supporting technical material.

  3. 03

    Follow the editorial process

    Review the current submission requirements and eligibility route before sending your work.

Editorial note: Confirm the journal's current eligibility and nomination rules before submission. This draft intentionally avoids promising acceptance or implying that any manuscript has already been peer reviewed.
