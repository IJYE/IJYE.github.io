01 / Aims & scope

Technical work deserves a rigorous public record.

IJYE is an open-access publication for engineering, software, and hardware architectures created by teenagers. We document how the system was conceived, built, tested, and understood.

Our scope favors depth over polish: architecture decisions, implementation traces, experimental evidence, and the lessons that can transfer to the next maker's project.

Format / 01

Open access

Readable without gatekeeping. Fully attributed. Built for reuse.

Format / 02

Architecture first

Methods, constraints, tradeoffs, and evidence are part of the record.

Format / 03

Youth-led

Teen authors retain ownership of the ideas and expertise they contribute.

03 / Eligibility

Work is the credential.

Submissions are gated by proof of work and program nomination, not institutional affiliation.

  1. 01

    Demonstrate the build

    Submit a reproducible artifact, repository, demonstration, or technical evidence of implementation.

  2. 02

    Receive program nomination

    Your work must be nominated through Haven or Fallen, as determined by the participating program.

  3. 03

    Enter peer review

    Eligible manuscripts are assessed for technical depth, reproducibility, clarity, and architectural rigor.
