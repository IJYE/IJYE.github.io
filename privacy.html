Privacy
Policy.

How IJYE handles information provided through this website and the submission form.

Effective date: October 30, 2026

The International Journal of Youth Engineering (IJYE) is operated by Scholars of Tomorrow Institute, which is fiscally sponsored by The Hack Foundation (“IJYE,” “we,” or “us”). This policy describes how IJYE handles information collected through this website and its submission process.

IJYE is based in Ohio, United States. The service providers described below may process information in other locations under their own privacy practices.

Information you provide

When you use the Tally submission form or contact us, you may provide information such as your name, contact details, age or eligibility information, project descriptions, answers to form questions, and files or links you choose to share. Please do not include sensitive personal information or information about another person unless you have permission and it is necessary.

How we use and share information

The IJYE editorial board can access submissions to administer and evaluate them, contact submitters, and decide whether work is selected for publication. If work is selected, IJYE may publish the approved work and the author details selected for publication. Submission details that are not approved for publication are not intentionally published by IJYE.

The submission form is hosted by Tally. Information entered into the form is processed by Tally and shared with IJYE. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request when your device connects to their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies: Tally, GitHub, and Google.

IJYE does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. This static website does not currently run first-party analytics. Third-party services may collect information as described in their policies.

Retention and publication

IJYE deletes rejected submissions from its working records after 30 days. Tally may retain submission data separately under its own terms and retention practices; IJYE does not control its independent retention or backups. Work selected for publication, along with approved author details, may remain publicly available as part of the journal. Public content may be copied, indexed, or archived by third parties.

Young submitters

IJYE is intended for people ages 13–18 and does not invite submissions from children under 13. If you are under 18, involve a parent or legal guardian and obtain their permission before submitting personal information or work. A parent or guardian may contact us with a privacy request.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you have provided to IJYE by emailing submissions@ijye.sotinstitute.org. We will review requests and respond as appropriate. A request cannot remove material already published from copies or archives controlled by third parties, and it does not control data held independently by Tally.

Security and changes

We use reasonable care in handling submissions, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy by posting a revised version here and changing the effective date. Questions about privacy can be sent to submissions@ijye.sotinstitute.org.
