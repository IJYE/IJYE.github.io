02 / THE SUBMISSION PROCESS

From manuscript
to editorial decision.

Planned workflow. Receipt does not guarantee review, acceptance, or publication.

STEP / 01

Prepare

Choose a pathway, prepare the PDF manuscript, and collect supporting files and project links.

STEP / 02

Submit

Complete the hosted intake form with author, project, manuscript, and declaration details.

STEP / 03

Editorial screening

An editor checks completeness, scope, attribution, and readiness for technical assessment.

STEP / 04

Review & decision

Eligible submissions may proceed to technical review. Authors are notified of the next step or decision.

03 / BEFORE YOU START

Have these ready.

  • Manuscript PDF: title, author names, abstract, problem, method/design, implementation, results, and limitations.
  • Supporting files: optional diagrams, figures, shareable datasets, build notes, test logs, or appendices.
  • Project links: repository, demo, documentation, or project page, if available.
  • Evidence: test results, measurements, screenshots, benchmarks, or other evidence for your claims.
  • Attribution: identify collaborators, third-party components, datasets, and sources; describe your contribution.
  • Permission: ensure you may share all files and remove passwords, API keys, private data, and secrets.
